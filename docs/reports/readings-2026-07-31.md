# Addendum 5 (same day): the reputation discount cracked — tier × band width (E-141 → FALSIFIED, E-142 opened)

the full band width — displayed as a % of band average (hence the per-ware
variation the player spotted), and applied **against** the player on buy
offers under the label "Discount offset". Twelve same-day readings reproduce
it to the cent (two factions, two tiers, ten wares, both sides, both books),
including the asymmetric food-rations band (width 17, not 2× the upper
spread) and "Allographyne" (khaakalloy band 1260/1400/1540; 0.15 × 280 = 42 →
1357.00 exact).

**The tiers themselves are real and stored**: the save carries one md-created
`<discount>` record per faction on the PLAYER component
(`md_relation_discount_1/2/3` = 0.05 / 0.15 / 0.25; on save_007 six factions
at 0.05, four at 0.15, one at 0.25 — Alliance's base +1). E-141's
"relation-scaled" hypothesis is FALSIFIED; what varied was the base, not the
rate. Dead ends passed through en route, kept for the record: flat %-of-avg
per tier (broken by GMJ's per-ware spread), relation-linear coefficients
(broken by teladi>holyorder inversion), rank quantization (the n/30 pattern
was tier × width/avg landing on round numbers).
