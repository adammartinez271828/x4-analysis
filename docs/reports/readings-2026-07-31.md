build method **closedloop** per the E-075 experiment):

| game time | 84,967.818 | 84,967.818 (paused) |

`<orders>` reads **60** where an outstanding-orders semantics would read 30 —
it is the TARGET. Three corroborating observations in the same pair:

1. **The input set-aside is visible and exact:** a `<supplies><wares>` row
   appeared — energy cells 3,120 — and station cargo dropped 19,092 → 15,972

   `desired = 90` = 30 × **3 hull parts each**. 104 EC + 3 hull parts is the
   **closedloop** cargo-drone recipe — the self-supply demand follows the
   station's *resolved build method* (override → closedloop), not the owner
   race's default. First direct evidence connecting E-069's `desired`
   semantics to the `v_build_method` resolution chain.
3. **The fresh offer prices at 240.5 Cr = (209 + 272)/2** — the band midpoint,
   E-129 confirmed on a newly-minted offer (its price was born on the rule,
   not converged onto it). Its `amount` is 0 while `desired` is 90 — amount
   had not yet ticked over at save time; treat `desired` as the need.

**The storage model is confirmed on all three** (to display rounding): the old
"1.21–1.30× allocation scale error" was pricing-side, exactly as the
cap-scope report concluded when it withdrew it — and the additive-modifier
panel rule (E-030) reproduces every quoted price.

**The player's mechanism — new, and it survives a 178-point test.** The LSO
shows NDE-080/KWC-232 as small net *producers* (+22.7k/h = 208.7k production
− 186k recycler draw), yet both are active *buyers*. The missing term is the
scrap processor's ~90k/h energy draw, which the LSO's consumption line omits
(the UI counterpart of E-045: processing modules have no timed cycle). With
it, their true net is −67.3k/h — net consumers — while CGW-678 (two EC
modules, +141k/h processor-inclusive) is the fleet's active seller.

Scored across all 13 corpus epochs, active energy-cell offer side vs the
sign of the net flow (rates: 208.7k/h per running EC module, 93k/h per
running recycler, 90k/h per processor; "running" = has a `<production>`
block and is not `waitingforresources`; corpus `station_module` deduped on
`entry_id`):

| consumption basis | correct | wrong | the misses |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **running modules** | **171/178** | 7 | all KWC-232 — a persistent buyer whose four recyclers read starved at most save instants (bursty scrap feed under-samples the duty cycle; recorded residual) |
| rated (all built modules) | 155/178 | 23 | MDS-738 + QIB-162 in **all 13 epochs** — persistent sellers with genuinely starved/idle recyclers; the rated basis is **rejected** |

Net consumers keep a **zero-amount sell** alongside the active buy (buy =
sell − 1 as always) — which is why earlier snapshot analyses, reading the
sell-side price, saw "sellers" everywhere. Registered as **E-140 CONFIRMED**
(the rule and the mechanism are the player's). The exact-digit allocations
were not captured (UI values quoted rounded); not added to the readings
fixture for that reason.
